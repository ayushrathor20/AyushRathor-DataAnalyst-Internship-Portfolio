# 🧪 Task 4 – Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

### Objective

Task 4 focused on communicating the findings from previous tasks as a business story and statistically validating a business hypothesis.

### Key Work

- Business storytelling
- Presentation development
- Key findings and recommendations
- Hypothesis formulation
- Statistical testing
- Welch's two-sample t-test
- p-value calculation
- 95% confidence interval
- Effect-size analysis
- Business interpretation

### Hypothesis Tested

**Business Question:**

Is there a statistically significant difference in mean customer-level revenue between male and female customers?

### Result

- **p-value:** 0.5235
- **95% Confidence Interval:** −₹21,042.49 to ₹10,714.21
- **Cohen's d:** −0.0415

### Conclusion

At the 5% significance level, the analysis does not provide sufficient statistical evidence to conclude that mean customer-level revenue differs between male and female customers.

### Tools

Python | Pandas | SciPy | Excel | PowerPoint | Statistical Analysis | Data Visualization
